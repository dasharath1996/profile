About Me

Hello! I'm Dasharath Kumar, a results-driven Data Engineer dedicated to architecting high-performance data pipelines, distributed systems, and enterprise-grade platforms.

My foundational technical journey began with a BCA from LNMCBM, followed by securing AIR 42 in NIMCET 2018, which earned me an MCA from NIT Trichy (2018–2021).

I kicked off my career at Comviva, scaling complex ETL workflows and high-volume distributed systems, which led to my promotion to Senior Data Engineer.

Currently, I am a Data Engineer at the National Australia Bank (NAB), responsible for delivering scalable data integration solutions, maximizing pipeline execution efficiency, and transforming complex enterprise data streams.
